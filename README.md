## 🔬 Scientific Methodology & pH Balancing

Standard detergent residues leave textile fibers alkaline (pH > 9.5), which attracts airborne particulate soil, hardens fibers, and accelerates textile wear. Our standardized methodology mandates:

1. **Fiber Pre-Inspection & Burn/Chemical Testing**: Differentiating natural cellulosic fibers (linen, cotton) from synthetic polymers (polyester, polyamide) and sensitive protein fibers.
2. **Encapsulation & Enzymatic Pre-Spray**: Breaking down sebum, grease, and pet stains with biodegradable surfactant solutions.
3. **Acidic Neutralization Extraction Rinse (pH 3.0 - 4.5)**: Cancelling out caustic salts, leaving fabrics tactilely soft and neutral (pH ~6.5 - 7.0).
4. **Dry Steam Thermal Shock (160°C at 9 Bar)**: Eliminating 99.9% of dust mites (*Dermatophagoides pteronyssinus*) and biological allergens in mattresses.
5. **Fluoropolymer Hydrophobic Shielding**: Applied to yachts, marine upholstery, and luxury villas to repel red wine, coffee, and UV salt mist degradation.
